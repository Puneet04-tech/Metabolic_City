H3 risk engine
risk.js
Deterministic and explainable.
  • Resolution 8 · 60-min window · 30-min half-life
  • R = 0.4·mobility + 0.4·climate + 0.2·vulnerability
  • Confidence, degradation flags, neighbour cascade
  • Bands: low <3 · moderate 3–5 · high 5–7 · critical ≥7
  • Alert synthesis + 3-hour forecast snapshots
